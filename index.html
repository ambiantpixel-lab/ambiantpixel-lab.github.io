FAQ

How do I install it on Windows?

Use Lively Wallpaper (free, Microsoft Store): “Add wallpaper”, then pick the .html file. With Wallpaper Engine (Steam), the Pack includes one ready-made Wallpaper Engine project (all formats): in Wallpaper Engine, choose Open Wallpaper → Open from File and import it. A Workshop release is coming soon.

And on a Mac?

The Pack includes a small Mac app that puts the wallpaper behind your desktop icons. The app is not notarized: on first launch, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”. You can also use the free app Plash with the .html file.

Can I just use a browser?

Yes. Open the .html file in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari and go full screen (F11, or Ctrl+Cmd+F on Mac). It works offline.

Does it work on my phone?

The Pack has stills and vertical videos sized for iPhone (1179×2556, 1290×2796) and Android (1440×3120). Android: use the video with any free “video live wallpaper” app. iPhone: iOS does not allow video wallpapers; use the stills, or turn the video into a Live Photo with a third-party app (limited effect).

Does it slow down my computer?

The scenes are drawn at a low internal resolution and a capped frame rate, then scaled up. Lively and Wallpaper Engine also pause it when a game or a full-screen app is running.

Is there a Steam Deck or ultrawide 5K2K version?

Yes. Each pack now includes 1280×800 for the Steam Deck, 5120×2160 (5K2K), 7680×1440 for three 16:9 screens (set your wallpaper app to “Span”), 16:10, MacBook and iPad sizes. Free update for people who already bought a pack.

Can I use it on stream or YouTube?

Yes, even monetized, as a background, with a credit “Wallpaper: Ambiant Pixel”. Resale and re-uploading the files are not allowed. Refunds within 14 days.
