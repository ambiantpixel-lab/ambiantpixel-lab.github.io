FAQ

How do I install it on Windows?

Use Lively Wallpaper (free, Microsoft Store): “Add wallpaper”, then pick the .html file. With Wallpaper Engine (Steam), the Pack includes a ready-made project for each format: open it from the editor or the Workshop.

And on a Mac?

The Pack includes a small Mac app that puts the wallpaper behind your desktop icons. The app is not notarised: on first launch, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”. You can also use the free app Plash with the .html file.

Can I just use a browser?

Yes. Open the .html file in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari and go full screen (F11, or Ctrl+Cmd+F on Mac). It works offline.

Does it work on my phone?

Android: yes, with the mobile video from the Pack and any free “video live wallpaper” app. iPhone: iOS does not allow video wallpapers; you can use the stills, or turn the video into a Live Photo with a third-party app (limited effect).

Does it slow down my computer?

The scenes are drawn at a low internal resolution and a capped frame rate, then scaled up. Lively and Wallpaper Engine also pause it when a game or a full-screen app is running.

Can I use it on stream or YouTube?

Yes, even monetised, as a background, with a credit “Wallpaper: Ambiant Pixel”. Resale and re-uploading the files are not allowed. Refunds within 14 days.
