FAQ

Does it support 3440×1440?

Yes, 3440×1440 is one of the native sizes in every theme pack, with day, dusk and night stills, videos and the live HTML version.

What about 2560×1080 or 5K2K?

5120×2160 (5K2K) is in every pack. On a 2560×1080 screen, the 3440×1440 file scales down cleanly.

Which app do I need?

Wallpaper Engine or Lively Wallpaper on Windows, the included app on Mac, or any browser in full screen.

Will it slow down games?

The scene runs at 30 fps at most, at low internal resolution. Lively and Wallpaper Engine pause it while a game is in full screen.
