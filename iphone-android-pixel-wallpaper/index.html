How to use them

Android: the vertical MP4 works with any free “video live wallpaper” app, which plays it on your home or lock screen. Or set a still as a normal wallpaper.

iPhone: iOS does not allow video wallpapers. Use the stills (a day one and a night one look great with iOS automatic switching), or turn the video into a Live Photo with a third-party app; the effect is limited.

The phone sizes are included in every theme pack and the Collection, next to the desktop live wallpapers for PC and Mac.

FAQ

Can I get the phone wallpapers for free?

The free download is desktop 16:9 only. The phone sizes come with the $6 theme packs.

Is it animated on iPhone?

Not really: iOS only accepts still images or Live Photos as wallpaper. On Android the vertical video plays as a live wallpaper.

Which iPhone sizes?

1179×2556 fits iPhone 14 Pro, 15, 15 Pro, 16 and similar; 1290×2796 fits the Plus and Pro Max models. Other phones scale cleanly.
