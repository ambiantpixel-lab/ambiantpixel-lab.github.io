Night Train: an animated pixel art train wallpaper

A train crosses a bay at the foot of a neon city. At night it is the Nocturne sleeper, blue and gold, with a dining car and a bar car; by day the Zephyr; sometimes a long freight train. Cars are put together at random, so no two trains are alike. Ferris wheel, lighthouse, ferry, real moon phase, rain and fog.
